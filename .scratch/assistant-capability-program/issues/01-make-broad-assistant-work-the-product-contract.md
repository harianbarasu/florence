# Make broad assistant work the product contract

## Question

How should `PLAN.md` and `AGENTS.md` make Florence a general noncoding household operator—rather than a Gmail/Calendar chatbot—while preserving the concrete two-adult family experience and requiring direct Pi/Hermes reuse?

## Answer

`PLAN.md` and `AGENTS.md` now define Florence as Instinct for a household. The capability order is Gmail attachments/all Calendars, maps/routes/time zones, live weather and flight help, reminder control, linked pages/PDFs, durable multi-step work, household dockets, communication and broader Workspace, browser/computer use, then concrete local-service and transaction workflows.

The contract keeps the behavior the user explicitly requested: ordinary language, no silence, real reactions/progress, complete 90-day Google accounting without immediate dumping, parental-unit knowledge, owner-first handling for a personal Calendar date, non-repetitive proactivity, and provider-confirmed results.

It requires direct Pi/Hermes reuse from the pinned commits and excludes their coding, shell, terminal, repository, and arbitrary-filesystem tools. It explicitly rejects generalized safety/privacy frameworks, speculative connector or approval systems, a second runtime or scheduler, and framework-only tests.
